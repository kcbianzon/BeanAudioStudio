How to Choose the Right Speaker or Party Sound Rental (Boston & RI)

When you need to hire a speaker or rent party speakers near you in Greater Boston or Rhode Island, start with your guest count and room size. Speaker Rentals FAQs

How much does it cost to rent party speakers or PA speakers in Boston?

Individual powered 12″ and 15″ PA speakers start at $65/day, stage monitors start at $55, and full turnkey sound packages with stands, cables, mixer, and wireless mics start at $199 (Small), $349 (Medium), and $880 (Large Line Array).

How many speakers do I need to rent for 100 to 200 guests?

For 50–150 guests (weddings, corporate town halls, receptions), we recommend our Medium Audio Package with four powered speakers on stands plus optional subwoofer support. For 150+ guests or live concerts, our Large Audio Package with line arrays and subs ensures even front-to-back coverage.

Do your Boston speaker rentals include delivery, stands, and cables?

Yes. Every speaker rental includes tripod stands, power cords, and XLR/Bluetooth interconnect cabling. You can choose self-pickup, white-glove delivery with setup and soundcheck across MA & RI, or full on-site technician operation.

Can I connect a phone, laptop, or DJ controller to your PA speakers?

Yes. All audio packages include a mixer with Bluetooth, AUX/3.5mm, RCA, and XLR inputs so iPhones, Androids, laptops, microphones, and DJ controllers plug right in without extra adapters.
