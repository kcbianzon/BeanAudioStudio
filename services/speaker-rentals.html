COMMON QUESTIONS

Speaker Rentals FAQs

How much does a speaker rental cost in Boston?

Individual powered PA speakers start at $65 per event day, stage monitors are $55, and complete 2-speaker PA packages with a mixer and wireless mic start at $199.

How many speakers do I need to rent for 100 to 200 guests?

For 50–150 guests (weddings, corporate town halls, receptions), we recommend our Medium Audio Package with four powered speakers on stands plus optional subwoofer support. For 150+ guests or live bands, our Large Audio Package with line arrays and subs ensures even coverage.

Do your Boston speaker rentals include delivery, stands, and cables?

Yes. Every speaker rental includes tripod stands, power cords, and XLR/Bluetooth interconnect cabling. You can choose pickup, white-glove delivery with setup and soundcheck, or full on-site technician operation.

Can I connect a phone, laptop, or DJ controller to your party speakers?

Yes. All speaker packages include a mixer with Bluetooth, AUX/3.5mm, RCA, and XLR inputs so phones, laptops, microphones, and DJ boards plug right in.
