COMMON QUESTIONS

Photo Booths FAQs

How do guests receive photos or 360 slow-motion videos?

Guests share captures instantaneously on-site via QR code, Apple AirDrop, SMS text, or email, plus unlimited high-speed 4×6 lab-quality photo prints on our Magic Mirror and Vintage Framed Mirror packages.

How much space and power does a 360 photo booth or Magic Mirror require?

We recommend an 8′×8′ to 10′×10′ flat floor area and access to a single standard 120V household outlet within 25 feet. Red carpet, velvet stanchions, and perimeter RGB LED lighting are included on select packages.
