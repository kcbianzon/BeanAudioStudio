Subwoofers FAQs

When should I rent a cardioid subwoofer like the QSC KS212C?

Cardioid subwoofers reduce rear bass radiation by 15 dB—ideal when DJs or live performers are close to a wall, beside sensitive stage mics, or in historic ballrooms with acoustic reverberation.
