Corporate Event AV FAQs

Do you provide dedicated on-site AV technicians for corporate meetings in Boston, MA?

Yes. You can book certified Beantown on-site AV technicians and audio engineers to manage morning setup, presenter mic checks, slide and laptop switching, Q&A panel levels, hybrid Zoom/Teams audio feeds, and quiet post-event strike.

We recommend our Presentation Package ($120) with two Shure QLX-D wireless lavalier or handheld mics for keynotes, paired with our Push-to-Talk (PTT) Conference Delegate gooseneck microphones ($35 each) for multi-speaker roundtables and town-hall Q&As.

Can you deliver to Seaport, Downtown Boston, Back Bay hotels, and Kendall Square?

Yes. We routinely deliver and set up corporate AV across Downtown Boston, Seaport District, Back Bay, Financial District, Cambridge/Kendall Square, Waltham, Burlington, and Greater New England, and accept corporate cards, ACH, and approved Net-30 invoicing.
