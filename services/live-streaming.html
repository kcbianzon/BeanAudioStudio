COMMON QUESTIONS

Live Streaming FAQs

What platforms do your live streaming setups support?

Our OBS/vMix workstations and hardware capture switchers output cleanly to Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Webex, YouTube Live, LinkedIn Live, Vimeo, and custom corporate RTMP portals while saving a high-bitrate local master recording.

What is the difference between the Basic ($621+) and Pro ($1,250+) Live Streaming Packages?

The Basic Package ($621+) includes 1 Sony 4K broadcast camera on a tripod, a 4K switcher, QSC TouchMix mixer, Shure wireless mic, and streaming workstation laptop. The Pro Package ($1,250+) upgrades to 3 Sony 4K camera angles (wide, tight presenter, and panel/audience), dual wireless mics, QSC TouchMix-16 console, and multi-angle hardware switching.
