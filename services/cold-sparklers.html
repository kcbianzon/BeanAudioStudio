COMMON QUESTIONS

Cold Sparklers FAQs

Are cold sparkler machines safe inside Boston hotels and wedding venues?

Yes. Cold spark fountains are non-pyrotechnic, cool to the touch moments after leaving the nozzle, produce zero flame, and emit virtually no smoke—making them approved at major Boston ballrooms and event halls.

How many cold spark machines do we need?

Two machines (Basic Package) are ideal for framing a DJ booth or wedding sweetheart table entrance. Four to six machines (Premium or Ultimate Packages) create dramatic symmetrical stage walls for concerts, corporate awards, and grand ballrooms.
