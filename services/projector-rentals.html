COMMON QUESTIONS

Projector Rentals FAQs

Do your Boston projector rentals include a screen and Mac/PC HDMI cables?

Yes. Our Basic and Mid-Size Video Packages bundle the laser projector, projection screen, long HDMI cabling (25ft–100ft), stand, and wireless slide clicker. You can also rent standalone HD projectors or 120″ Fast-Fold screens individually.

When should we choose a projector vs a 75″ TV or LED video wall?

For darkened or lightly lit breakouts and banquets up to 175 guests, a laser projector and 120″ Fast-Fold screen offer unmatched screen size per dollar. For sun-drenched trade show floors or high ambient brightness, consider 75″ 4K TVs or an Indoor LED Video Wall.
