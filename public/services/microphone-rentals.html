COMMON QUESTIONS

Microphones FAQs

Which microphone is best for a conference panel or keynote speaker?

Our Presentation Setup pairs two Shure QLX-D wireless lavalier or Countryman headset mics with a dual receiver and compact mixer for hands-free corporate talks, while Wireless Handhelds excel for audience Q&A.

Do microphone rentals include stands, receivers, and XLR cables?

Yes. Every mic rental includes matching wireless receiver units, fresh batteries/power supplies, mic clips, stands where applicable, and XLR patch cabling.
