COMMON QUESTIONS

Indoor LED Walls FAQs

What pixel pitch is best for an indoor ballroom conference or gala?

Our 2.67mm fine-pitch indoor panels deliver razor-sharp text, financial slides, and 1080p/4K video for guests seated as close as 8–10 feet away—ideal for Boston corporate keynotes, BCEC trade show booths, and luxury wedding backdrops.

What comes included with the 12′×7′ and 16′×9′ Indoor LED Wall packages?

Both turnkey packages include the 2.67mm modular LED cabinet array, NovaStar VX1000 processor with HDMI/SDI inputs, freestanding ground-support frame, 2 powered PA speakers, audio mixer, 1 Shure wireless handheld mic, delivery, setup, and teardown.

What power is required for an indoor LED video wall?

A 12′×7′ LED wall typically operates across two dedicated 120V 20A circuits, while a 16′×9′ or larger custom wall uses three 20A circuits or a venue 3-phase power drop. Our team coordinates power distribution directly with your Boston venue engineer.
