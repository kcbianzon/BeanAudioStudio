COMMON QUESTIONS

Wedding Sound & AV FAQs

What sound equipment do I need to rent for a wedding ceremony and reception?

For the ceremony (up to 150 guests), we recommend our Small Audio Package ($199+) paired with a Shure QLX-D clip-on wireless lavalier mic ($65) for the officiant and a wireless handheld mic for readings. For the reception (50–150+ guests), our Medium Audio Package ($349+) or Standard DJ Package ($499+) with four speakers, wireless toast mics, and an 18-inch subwoofer covers speeches and dancing cleanly.

Can we plug Spotify or a laptop directly into your wedding speaker rentals?

Yes. Every wedding sound package includes an audio mixer with Bluetooth, 3.5mm/AUX, RCA, and balanced XLR inputs so playlists from an iPhone, iPad, MacBook, or DJ controller play effortlessly.

Are your Cold Sparklers and Dancing on a Cloud dry ice machines safe for indoor wedding venues?

Yes. Our Cold Sparkler machines use non-flammable composite granules that produce zero open flame and virtually no smoke, while our Chauvet Nimbus dry ice fog machine uses real solid CO2 and heated water so thick white cloud stays strictly at ankle height without rising into ceiling smoke sensors.

Do you deliver and set up wedding sound and lighting at Boston and Newport venues?

Yes. Our technicians handle early venue load-in, neat cable taping, frequency coordination, soundcheck, and post-reception teardown across Boston hotels, historic estates, tents, Cape Cod, and Newport/Providence, RI.
