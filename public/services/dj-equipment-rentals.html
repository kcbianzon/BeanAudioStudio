DJ Equipment FAQs

Can I rent just a DJ board or individual Pioneer CDJ-3000 players?

Yes. You can rent a single Pioneer DDJ-1000SRT controller ($90), individual CDJ-3000 ($125) or CDJ-2000NXS2 ($95) decks, mixers (DJM-900NXS, DJM-S9, Rane 72), or Technics 1200 turntables separately or bundled with PA speakers.

Are Pro DJ Link Ethernet cables, slipmats, and RCA/XLR cables included?

Yes. Every DJ equipment rental includes Pro DJ Link networking cables, shielded RCA/XLR interconnects, slipmats and headshells for Technics 1200s, and power supplies.

Do your DJ packages include speakers and delivery in Greater Boston?

Our Basic ($299), Standard ($449), and Pro ($699) DJ packages include speakers and stands, and you can select warehouse pickup or turnkey delivery and setup anywhere in Boston, Cambridge, or New England.
