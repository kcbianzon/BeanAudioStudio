COMMON QUESTIONS

Projector Rentals FAQs

How bright a projector do I need for a hotel ballroom or conference room?

We recommend 4,500 lumens for dimmed breakout rooms up to 40 guests, and our 6,000+ lumen laser projector (Mid-Size Video Package) for larger ballrooms and 50–175 guest events with ambient light.

Do Boston projector rentals include Mac/PC HDMI cables and stands?

Yes. Standalone projectors include 50ft HDMI cabling and power cords, while Basic ($299) and Mid-Size ($499) Video Packages include the projection screen, stand, long-run HDMI cables, and wireless presentation clicker.

When should we choose a projector vs a 75″ 4K TV or LED wall?

Choose a laser projector + 120″ Fast-Fold screen for maximum screen size per dollar indoors. Choose Samsung 75″ 4K TVs or a 2.67mm Indoor LED Wall for bright sunlit atriums and crowded trade show aisles.
