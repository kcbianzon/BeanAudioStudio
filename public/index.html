What equipment is available in your Boston rental inventory?

Our Boston rental inventory includes small, medium, and large PA audio packages; powered speakers and subwoofers; Shure wireless microphones; Behringer and Allen & Heath mixers; Pioneer DJ CDJs and packages; HD laser projectors, Samsung 65-inch and 75-inch 4K TVs, indoor 2.67mm LED walls, and outdoor LED trailers; inflatable outdoor movie screens; 360 and Magic Mirror photo booths; 50–300 silent disco headphone packs; wireless uplighting; cold sparklers; dry ice machines; and live streaming kits.

Do you handle delivery, setup, soundcheck, and pickup?

Yes. We provide turnkey delivery, cabling, room tuning, soundcheck, and pickup across Greater Boston and all of New England, plus self-pickup options for compact gear and dedicated on-site technician support.

Are Cold Sparklers and Dry Ice fog safe for indoor Boston venues?

Yes. Our Cold Sparkler machines use non-pyrotechnic composite granules with cool sparks, no open flame, and virtually no smoke, while Dry Ice machines keep chilled white fog strictly low to the floor.

What areas in Massachusetts and New England do you service?

We cover Boston proper (Back Bay, Seaport, Downtown, South End, Fenway), Cambridge, Somerville, Brookline, Newton, Waltham, Worcester, Cape Cod, Providence RI, Southern NH, and New England.
