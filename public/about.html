Our Story & Event Production Philosophy

Beantown Audio Rentals was founded to replace confusing technical jargon and unreliable rental gear with tested, commercial-grade equipment and transparent support. Whether you are planning a corporate town hall in Boston's Seaport, a panel or commencement in Cambridge, a Back Bay ballroom wedding, a block party in Somerville, or an outdoor movie screening in Massachusetts or Rhode Island, we treat every show—big or small—with the same precision.

“An event feels calm when someone has already met its worst day. At Beantown, we prepare for every variable so your event runs effortlessly.” — Calvin Reid, Founder

Our inventory covers complete PA audio systems, powered speakers, 18″ subwoofers, wireless & delegate push-to-talk microphones, corporate meetings AV support, Behringer X32 & Allen & Heath digital mixers, Pioneer CDJ-3000s & DJ packages, HD laser projectors & Fast-Fold screens, 65″ & 75″ 4K TV displays, indoor 2.67mm LED video walls, outdoor LED trailers, inflatable movie screens with projector & speakers, 360 & Magic Mirror photo booths, silent disco packs, wireless uplighting, indoor-safe cold sparklers, dry ice cloud fog, and multi-camera live streaming kits.

Every microphone, powered speaker, projector, LED cabinet, and cable undergoes bench inspection between events so cables tape flat and shows run on time. Need guidance sizing your venue or booking delivery? Call or text our Boston desk daily at (978) 601-5473 or email info@beantownaudiorentals.com.
